🤲 Forgiveness Duas

Dua 1

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَتُبْ عَلَيَّ إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ التَّوَّابُ الرَّحِيمُ

English meaning:

My Lord, forgive me and accept my repentance. Indeed, You are the Acceptor of Repentance, the Most Merciful.

Source: Abu Dawud and Jami' al-Tirmidhi (authentic hadith).

Dua 2

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي ذَنْبِي كُلَّهُ دِقَّهُ وَجِلَّهُ وَأَوَّلَهُ وَآخِرَهُ وَعَلاَنِيَتَهُ وَسِرَّهُ

English meaning:

O Allah, forgive all my sins — the small and the great, the first and the last, the public and the private.

Source: Sahih Muslim.

👨‍👩‍👧 Duas for Parents

Dua 1 (Qur'an 17:24)

رَبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا

English meaning:

My Lord, have mercy upon them as they raised me when I was young.

Source: Qur'an 17:24.

Dua 2 (Qur'an 14:41)

رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ الْحِسَابُ

English meaning:

Our Lord, forgive me, my parents, and the believers on the Day when the account is established.

Source: Qur'an 14:41.

🌿 Duas for Anxiety & Peace of Mind

Dua 1 (Qur'an 2:286)

رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذْنَا إِن نَّسِينَا أَوْ أَخْطَأْنَا

English meaning:

Our Lord, do not hold us accountable if we forget or make a mistake.

Source: Qur'an 2:286.

Dua 2 (Authentic Hadith)

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ

English meaning:

O Allah, I seek refuge in You from anxiety and sorrow.

Source: Sahih al-Bukhari.
